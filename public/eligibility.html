Can I apply?

How we choose students.

We consider your family's income, family size, school costs, and academic effort. Income matters most, but we also weigh several other factors.

40%

Family income

How much your family earns each month.

15%

Family size

A larger family can mean greater financial need.

15%

School costs

Tuition, exam fees, transport, books, uniforms, and internet access.

15%

Family hardship

Extra consideration for hardships such as losing a parent, a serious illness or disability in the family, or losses from a disaster.

10%

Parent's work

An unstable job, daily-wage work, or unemployment.

5%

School work

Regular attendance and good academic effort.

Income levels

Household income guide

  • Under PKR 45,000: highest priority
  • PKR 45,001-60,000: high priority
  • PKR 60,001-80,000: some priority
  • PKR 80,001-100,000: small chance
  • Over PKR 100,000: usually not eligible, unless there is serious hardship

Papers needed

What to email us

After you submit your application, email your documents as PDF files to successscholarships2026@gmail.com, with your Application ID in the subject line so we can match them to your file.

  • CNIC or B-Form and Family Registration Certificate
  • Income certificate, salary slip, and utility bill
  • Admission letter, fee challan, and attendance record
  • Teacher recommendation letter
  • Disability certificate, death certificate, or BISP/Zakat registration if needed
Email PDFs after applying
